two existing projects and Assura itself, and prepare an evidence-backed backlog
that can be entered in Beads with real dependencies.

- Delegate two independent existing-project trials using disposable copies.

- Deliver a review and structured backlog with priority, evidence, acceptance
  criteria, dependency direction, and a clear first ready set.
