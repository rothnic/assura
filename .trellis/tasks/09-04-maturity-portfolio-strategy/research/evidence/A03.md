   ambiguous repository remains reversible, creates no package manifest, and

## Independent review and repair

Independent review of `origin/master...d3d15d8` found four valid issues:
multiple manifests were silently prioritized, the serialized onboarding state
changed under the v1 schema, conflict objects were not structurally validated,
and the unavailable A01 guidance dimension prevents A03 packet acceptance.

The repaired candidate now detects multiple Cargo/package/pyproject manifests
as `ambiguous`, records each manifest as a structured conflict, and emits
`conflict_requires_user` without overwriting policy. It validates `kind`,
`source`, and `detail` for every conflict. The JSON report schema is explicitly
`assura.agent-onboarding.v2`; tests, the published onboarding guide, and the
generated website demo are updated to describe the expanded specialization
states. The A01 guidance limitation remains an open, non-passing acceptance
boundary and is not hidden by the schema update.
