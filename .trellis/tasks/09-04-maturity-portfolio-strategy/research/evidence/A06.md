## Implemented and negative controls

- Codex hook rendering is bounded to a UTF-8-safe 2 KiB body with a closing
  marker and full JSONL pointer; the existing multibyte hook contract remains
  covered by `agent_surface_cli`.
- Critical feedback wins over high feedback, and cooldown identity includes the
  configured policy path and raw policy bytes.
- Identical unchanged findings remain suppressed during cooldown. A clean
  observation evicts only the resolved fingerprint, so a reintroduced finding
  re-emits even when another finding on the same path persists. A pathless
  daemon failure is likewise evicted on healthy observation and re-emits if it
  recurs.
